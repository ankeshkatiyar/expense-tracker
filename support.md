# Support

Need help or found a bug? Email
<a href="mailto:{{ site.support_email }}">{{ site.support_email }}</a>. We
usually reply within 2 working days. You can also use **Settings → Contact
support** in the app, which adds your app version automatically.

## Common questions

**I didn't get my sign-in code.**
Check your spam or promotions folder. Codes expire after a few minutes, so
request a new one if needed and use the latest email.

**How do I add expenses from bank SMS?**
Copy the bank message and open the app. It will offer to add the transaction.
You can also set up a shortcut with the Shortcuts app.

**How do I share expenses with my family?**
Go to the Family tab, create a family and invite members by username. Mark any
expense as "Hide from family" to keep it private.

**How do I export my data?**
Settings → Export all as CSV.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all data
from our servers.
