# Delete your {{ site.app_name }} account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open {{ site.app_name }} on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone.
2. Go to **Settings → Delete account**.
3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email
<a href="mailto:{{ site.support_email }}?subject=Delete%20my%20account">{{ site.support_email }}</a>
from the email address you sign in with, with the subject **"Delete my account"**.
We will confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

- Your account, email address, username and name
- All your expenses, income, recurring payments, budgets and settings
- Your receipt photos
- Your family membership and any bill splits you are part of

## What we keep

- Diagnostic (crash) reports contain no expense data. They are unlinked from you
  when you delete your account, and all reports are deleted after 12 months.
- Support emails are kept for up to 2 years so we can follow up on your request.
